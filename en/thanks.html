Get started

  1. Install PenWeb from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open any page or PDF and use the floating button to draw, highlight and annotate.
  3. To use AI, open AI Settings, create a free key with your preferred provider and save it.

Your key is stored only in your browser. PenWeb adds no markup on top of usage: you pay the provider the real cost of the model you pick, when applicable.

What already works

  • Pressure-sensitive pen, highlighter, lasso selection and eraser on any website;
  • Chat with the page, including text, screenshots, region capture and vision over your drawing;
  • Page summary, flashcards and editable mind maps built from your highlights;
  • Export maps as PNG, autosave of drawings and screen-reader support.
