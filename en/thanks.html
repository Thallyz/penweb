In the meantime you can already get your key ready. 1.5.5 is a bring your own key build: the AI talks straight from your browser to OpenRouter, and PenWeb never limits credits for users on their own key — forever.

How to create your OpenRouter key (2 minutes)

  1. Create an account at openrouter.ai.
  2. In the menu open Keys and click Create Key.
  3. Copy it right away: it is shown only once. Lose it? Just create another.
  4. Add credits on OpenRouter (US$ 5 already goes a long way for studying).
  5. In PenWeb: side panel → AI tab → provider OpenRouter → paste the key → Save.

Your key is stored only in your browser. PenWeb adds no markup on top of usage: you pay OpenRouter the real cost of the model you pick.
