Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Ler em português

PenWeb is a local-first extension. The developer does not run a server that receives your content and does not use your data for advertising, profiling or sale. Some AI features send content to the API of the provider you choose, only when you trigger an action and with your own API key.

1. Summary

  • Stored in your browser: highlights, notes, drawings, maps, flashcards, chat history, context summaries, recent attachments, preferences and API keys.
  • Sent only when you use AI: the content needed for the task (text, highlights, page content, previous messages and attached images) goes directly from your browser to the chosen provider.
  • We do not: require a PenWeb account, use analytics, show ads, sell data or upload content in the background.

2. Definitions

For this policy:

  • PenWeb / Extension: the Chrome application distributed through the Chrome Web Store and its official website.
  • DWLabs / Developer: the party responsible for PenWeb, based in Brazil.
  • User / you: the person who installs or uses PenWeb.
  • Device: the computer and browser where the extension is installed.
  • Personal Data: information that identifies or can identify a person, which may be contained in pages, highlights, notes or images.
  • AI Provider: the service you choose to process AI requests, such as OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq or DeepSeek; Ollama runs locally.
  • Usage Data: technical information automatically generated by third-party services, such as GitHub Pages access logs.
  • Third-Party Service: an external service used for hosting, contact, payment or AI.

3. Data stored in your browser

PenWeb uses chrome.storage.local and the browser's local storage to keep, on your device:

  • highlights: text, page title and URL, color, note and AI-generated explanations;
  • drawings, autosaves and saved sessions;
  • mind maps, flashcards and preferences for theme, language and zoom;
  • per-page chat history, context summary and recent attachments; older images may be removed automatically to save space;
  • the selected provider, model and API key.

This data is not synced to PenWeb servers. If you clear the extension data, delete conversations/notes or uninstall PenWeb, it is removed from your browser.

4. How we use data

  • Local data: used only to provide the features you trigger in the browser — drawing, highlighting, annotating, searching, reviewing and chatting with AI.
  • Content sent to AI: used to produce the response you request from the chosen provider. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • Website form: the email address and stated purpose are used to respond to your request and send PenWeb updates.
  • Donations: Pix and Mercado Pago links open external services; PenWeb does not receive card data.

5. AI features and providers

AI features are optional. They only work when you configure an API key for a compatible provider — currently OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq or DeepSeek. Ollama, when configured, runs locally on your computer. The key is stored locally and is sent only to the chosen provider in the authentication header of the request.

When you trigger an AI action (chat, summary, mind map, flashcards, highlight explanation or image analysis), PenWeb sends directly from your browser to the provider's API the content needed to fulfill the request, which may include:

  • your question and the system instructions;
  • selected text, highlights and notes;
  • the active page's title, URL and text;
  • previous chat messages and the context summary;
  • images, screenshots or crops you attach to the request;
  • the model identifier and technical metadata for the call.

PenWeb does not sit in that path with its own servers and does not receive a copy of the content sent. The chosen provider handles the data under its own privacy policy and terms, which may include retention for security and use for training depending on the model and plan (including free plans). We do not control those practices. Use AI features only with content you have the right to share and avoid sending sensitive data.

6. Images, screenshots and screen capture

Region crops, pasted screenshots and images selected from your computer are processed locally in the browser. An image is sent to an AI provider only if you attach it to a message and send the request. Region capture and PNG export are not sent to PenWeb servers; export creates a local file for download.

7. Permissions used

  • activeTab / scripting: to run the pen, highlighter and page features only when you activate PenWeb.
  • Access to all sites (<all_urls>): required for the floating launcher and to draw, highlight and annotate on any page. We do not use this permission to build a browsing history or to read pages in the background. The active URL is used locally to associate notes and conversations; when you use AI, it may be sent to the provider along with the request content.
  • storage: to keep notes, maps, flashcards, history and preferences in your browser.
  • sidePanel: to show the side panel with chat, highlights and the mind map editor.
  • contextMenus: to add PenWeb actions to the right-click menu.
  • downloads: to save exported images and maps to your computer.
  • Provider API access: to send the AI requests you initiate.

8. Sharing and what we do not do

We share content only with the AI provider you choose when you trigger an AI feature, and with the services needed for the website (GitHub Pages and FormSubmit for hosting and contact) or for a donation you start (Mercado Pago). We may disclose form data if legally required or to protect rights and safety. We do not sell, rent or trade data; we do not use analytics, tracking pixels, ads or advertising profiles; we do not execute remote code.

Use of data obtained through Chrome APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The extension does not use tracking cookies or advertising. The website is static, has no ads and does not use analytics. GitHub Pages may log technical access data under its own policy. The contact form uses FormSubmit to deliver the message; that service may use cookies or technologies necessary for operation and spam protection, under its own policy. AI and payment providers may also use their own technologies when you access those services.

10. Legal bases, rights and control (LGPD)

Local processing happens on your device and under your control. Sending data to an AI provider happens at your initiative when you use an AI feature; the website form is used to respond to your request and send updates you asked for. Where applicable, we process data based on consent, performance of the requested service, compliance with legal obligations or the legitimate interest of responding and keeping the service secure.

Under the Brazilian LGPD (Law 13.709/2018), you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, portability, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent. Because local data stays in your browser, you control it directly through the panel options or by clearing the extension data. For form data, write to the contact below; for data sent to an AI provider, use that provider's controls and privacy channel. If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom, you may also have equivalent rights under local law.

11. Retention

Local data remains in your browser until you delete it. Form data is kept for as long as needed to respond to the request and send the requested updates, or until you ask for removal. The AI provider sets its own retention period; GitHub Pages, FormSubmit and Mercado Pago keep logs and data under their own policies. We do not keep a remote copy of your local data for restoration.

12. International transfers

AI providers, GitHub Pages, FormSubmit and payment services may process data outside Brazil. By using an AI feature, submitting the form or starting a donation, you direct the data to the chosen service, which applies the safeguards described in its own policy. Where required, PenWeb uses contractual mechanisms or applicable legal bases for processing under its control.

13. Security

Keys and local data are stored in the extension storage in your Chrome profile. We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, but no system is completely immune to failure. Protect access to your computer and browser account; if you use an API key, revoke it at the provider if you suspect exposure.

14. Children and minors

PenWeb is not directed to children and does not require an account. We do not intentionally collect children's data through the website. If a guardian believes a child submitted data through the form, contact us so we can remove the information. Use of AI features by minors must follow the chosen provider's rules and minimum age.

15. Links to third-party sites

The extension and website contain links to AI providers, donation services, GitHub and other external pages. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Read their policies before sending data or completing a donation.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PenWeb gains new features or providers. The date at the top indicates the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on this page; continued use of the extension after an update means you are aware of the revised version.

17. Contact

Questions about this policy or access/removal requests? Contact us at dwlabs.penweb@gmail.com.
